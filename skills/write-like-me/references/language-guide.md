# Language guide

Read this reference when choosing greetings, sign-offs, or wording, or when the requested tone is uncertain.

## Greetings

- `Hi [Name],` is the default.
- `Hey [Name],` is appropriate for known colleagues and informal contacts.
- `Hi all,` works for groups.
- `Dear [Name],` is reserved for unfamiliar or notably formal external contacts.
- Omit the greeting only in a rapid internal back-and-forth.

After the greeting, use no more than one sentence of acknowledgment, thanks, apology, or context:

- `Thanks for reaching out.`
- `Thanks for pulling this together.`
- `I appreciate the context.`
- `Apologies for the delay on this.`

Do not default to `I hope this email finds you well`.

## Closings and sign-offs

Use one purposeful closing line when needed:

- `Let me know if you have any issues.`
- `Let me know what works best.`
- `Happy to adjust if needed.`
- `Would be great to hear your thoughts.`
- `Open to hear other views on this.`

Then choose one short sign-off:

- `Best,` for most professional correspondence
- `Best regards,` for more formal external correspondence
- `Thanks` for quick internal or scheduling replies
- Sofiane's first name alone, or no sign-off, for fast internal threads

Do not stack several generic closing lines.

## Preferred language

Use naturally where appropriate, but do not force these phrases into every message:

- `Thanks for...`
- `I appreciate...`
- `Apologies for...`
- `Let me know if...`
- `Happy to...`
- `Works for me`
- `What works best`
- `One thing to consider, though...`
- `My instinct is...`
- `Would be great to hear your thoughts`

## Avoid

Avoid:

- Em dashes and emoji
- Multiple exclamation marks (one mild exclamation mark is acceptable when genuinely warm, especially in a thank-you or resolved support exchange)
- Long throat-clearing introductions
- Dense paragraphs
- Repeated apologies
- Stacked hedges such as `I just wanted to maybe check`
- Corporate filler such as `circling back`, `touching base`, `synergy`, `moving the needle`, or `per my last email`
- Generic AI closings such as `Please do not hesitate to reach out if you have any questions`
- Excessive enthusiasm or flattery
- Restating the entire request in the final paragraph
- Unnecessary headings or heavy formatting inside ordinary emails
