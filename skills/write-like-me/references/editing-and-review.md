# Editing and review

Read this reference when rewriting an existing draft or when the user asks for a tone/style edit.

## Rewrite workflow

1. Preserve all facts, commitments, dates, links, and names.
2. Identify the single main purpose.
3. Remove throat-clearing and generic AI language.
4. Move the main point into the first two or three sentences.
5. Split multiple actions or options into bullets.
6. Reduce repeated thanks, apologies, and closing phrases.
7. Match the greeting and sign-off to the relationship.
8. Return only the finished draft unless Sofiane asks for commentary.

Do not make the draft blunter merely to make it shorter. Preserve necessary warmth and political judgment. Do not invent facts, commitments, or certainty while improving the prose.

## Review checklist

Before returning a draft, confirm:

- The purpose is clear within the first two or three sentences.
- There is at most one sentence of preamble or acknowledgment.
- Paragraphs are short and multiple items are in bullets.
- Dates, times, amounts, names, links, and actions are specific and preserved.
- An apology is brief and used only once.
- Any disagreement is clear but constructive, with the tradeoff or recommendation visible.
- The closing is purposeful and singular.
- The formality fits the recipient.
- There are no em dashes, emoji, corporate filler, generic AI phrasing, excessive enthusiasm, or stacked hedges.
- The result sounds natural rather than polished by committee.

Return the finished draft without explaining the style choices unless asked.
